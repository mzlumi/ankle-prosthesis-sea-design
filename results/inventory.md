## Sign convention check

Mean right ankle moment, angular velocity and power over the samples at 45 to 60 % of the gait cycle (from `gcRight/HeelStrike`) that have a moment (the foot on a force plate), split by the steady locomotion label (treadmill: belt above 0.4 m/s). This is late stance, where the plantarflexors push off. With the OpenSim convention (dorsiflexion positive), push-off in walking, ramp ascent and stair ascent should show a negative (plantarflexion) moment, a negative (plantarflexing) velocity and a positive power. Ramp and stair descent are listed for comparison: there the ankle mostly absorbs energy.
